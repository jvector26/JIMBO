    How it works. Each player's rating is points per 100 possessions over an average player: box-score evidence for twelve skills plus hustle stats (loose balls, 3-point contests), weighted by how much each has historically moved scoring margin, plus what lineup results show beyond the box score (counted at 0.6 on offense and 0.8 on defense by default, where past seasons predicted best), then adjusted for age (the age curve has its own slider). Minutes come from each player's history and age; team minutes always total 82 games of 240. The forecast blends the model with the betting market and roster age, the mix that best predicted 330 past preseasons, with the model and market shares adding to 100% (average miss 8.2 wins, against 8.4 for the betting line alone and 8.7 for the model alone). Weight sliders change the model; at the default mix the forecast moves about 0.35 wins for every model win (the model-vs-Vegas slider changes that).
